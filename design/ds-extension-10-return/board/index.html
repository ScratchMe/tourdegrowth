The marketplace — every state

Brief 10's states, drawn from the components' own source, every figure computed by one replica of the marketplace's money (market.js, checked by market-check.mjs against the brief's table). Each opens at its width (390 or 1280), in English or French, in the “products” or the “services” words; every one exists in all of them from the same components.

Serve this folder over http (ES modules do not load from file://), e.g. python3 -m http.server from the project root, then open design/ds-extension-10-return/board/index.html. “Key” marks the states the brief asks for (FR 1280, EN 390); “measures” a screen drawn only to measure.

# INVENTORY — where every figure of the marketplace goes

*Extension 10. Every figure of brief 10's "What the model computes, per
side" and of the total, with its place on the board, in the "What if?"
card, in the opened panel and on each slide — or "not shown" and why. This
file is how Antoine checks that nothing is shown twice, lost, or set
against the other side. Figures: the example of §22.10 (brief 10's table),
as the board prints them in French; today → with the example's what-ifs.*

## The rules

**Once per surface.** A surface is what one person sees at once: the board
with one side shown (its card included), the board with the panel opened,
each slide. A figure is printed in one place per surface; where it appears
a second time, the line says why. Inside one component, a sentence and the
chart that draws it count once (MoneyBlock's finding and its WorthBars; a
slide's tiles and its PaybackChart).

**One side at a time (C70).** A side's figures appear only under the
selector, with that side shown, and on that side's slides. The only places
where both sides' figures meet are the **total** — TotalBand above the
selector, and slide № 1 — and both are **sums**: the two streams and their
total, never a ratio, a share, a bar or a rank.

**Each stream's own margin (constraint 5).** The buyer's LTV, payback and
cash use the commissions' margin (55–65 %); the paid seller's, the
subscriptions' (85 %). One is never used for the other; without its own
margin, a side's figure is "?".

Three deliberate exceptions, each said where it happens:

1. **Typed inputs** stay in "Your numbers", as every typed number does (the
   take rate, the order value, the frequency, the price…). The money reads
   them; it prints only the ones it explains (what one active buyer brings,
   the cost of a paid seller).
2. **A curve's origin** is labelled (« 32 854 € aujourd'hui ») on the card,
   where the band above already prints the same stream's month: a chart
   needs its starting point (as extension 09).
3. **The diagnosis and its slide restate the target** they compare (« 9 %,
   sous ta cible (12 %) »): a comparison needs both terms.

Legend: **Band** TotalBand (shared, above the selector) · **B** the board,
the side shown (MoneyBlock unless said) · **C** its card (LeverCard) ·
**P** its panel, opened · **F** its funnel card (SideFunnel) · **L** "Your
numbers" · slides **№ 1** total · **№ 2–5** demand: funnel, leak,
"What if?", unit economics · **№ 6–9** supply: the same.

## Not shown anywhere, on purpose

- **A comparison between the two sides** (C70): no "which side first", no
  ratio, no share of the total, no chart with both sides on one axis, no
  slide that sets them side by side. The selector's options carry no figure
  and no colour; the list's side tags carry no colour.
- **A reference of any kind** (C73): no "commonly cited" payback or
  LTV:CAC; the PaybackChart is drawn with `reference: null`; no tile note
  situates a ratio.
- **Red for anything but the leak**: the projections, the gains, the
  "?" boxes, the side notes, the "not enough targets" state, the other
  stages under their targets — all ink.
- **Anything computed on the other stream's margin** (constraint 5).
- **A zero for a stream that does not exist**: without the subscriptions,
  supply's money is said in words.

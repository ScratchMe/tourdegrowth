SideFunnel — design system extension 10. One side's funnel, the board's one raised card.

Questions 3 and 4. The peloton (return 07, screenshot 05) is the engine's
way of showing a funnel: 100 dots, a column per stage counted **on the same
100**, the referred as rings, the upstream line. A marketplace keeps that
grammar whole, **for one side at a time** — never both sides on one axis
(C70). It is a new component, not a `Peloton` with fewer columns, because
the peloton is not in the synced system and a side needs three things it
has not: a second base, monthly rates, and an aside.

## Demand (the buyers)

- Three columns on the same 100 buyer sign-ups: the 100 (with the 8
  referred as rings, DotGrid `referred`), first order (« Première commande
  sous 30 jours »), second order (« Deuxième commande sous 90 jours »).
- **Liquidity on its own base**, after a rule, under its own heading:
  « Liquidité · sur 100 recherches ? » (or « demandes envoyées »: the kind
  is the heading's word), the fill rate as a column of 100, and the given
  sentence under it (« Taux de service : 9 % des recherches aboutissent à
  une commande »). It is not a fourth stage of the sign-ups — a search is
  not a person — so it never sits in their row of columns without the
  rule and the heading. The note says it: « une autre base, pas les mêmes
  personnes ».
- The fill rate is the leak in the example: its dots red (DotGrid
  `highlighted`) and the alert Tag « Sous la cible ». That is the side's
  one red; the first order, below its target too but not the named stage,
  stays ink.

## Supply (the sellers)

- The 100 seller sign-ups, first sale (« Première vente sous 60 jours »),
  subscribed (« Abonnés sous 90 jours »). Without the subscriptions: the
  100 and the first sale only — two columns at the left, never stretched.
- **The two churns are rates, not columns**: under the columns, « Chaque
  mois », « Vendeurs actifs qui partent 3 % », « Vendeurs payants qui
  résilient 3 % · cible 2,5 % ». A monthly rate drawn as dots on 100
  sign-ups would lie (they are not the same people over the same time).
- Without the subscriptions, `aside`: « Leur travail se lit dans le taux de
  service, côté demande : 9 % des recherches aboutissent à une commande. »
  — the one place the board says where supply shows. A pointer, never a
  comparison.

## Rules

- A column's width never grows past the peloton's (`--market-funnel-col`,
  four to the card): one or two columns stay at the left.
- Every column's head has the same height (`--market-funnel-head`), so the
  grids start on one line, whatever the label's length in either
  vocabulary.
- On a phone each column is a row: the grid at the left (118px), its words
  at the right — the peloton's phone layout.
- `medium="slide"`: the deck's 1 920 canvas, the slide type tokens, no card
  (the slide is the frame), the rates at the columns' right, the legend in
  a line. The slide's title is the side's verdict, its kicker the side.
- No figure is computed here: the board passes counts already brought back
  to 100. A stage not measured is DotGrid's "?" box, never an empty column.

SideNote — design system extension 10. A side's absent part, said in words.

Question 11. Supply without the subscriptions has no money, one funnel
column and, in the example, "not enough targets". It must not look broken
or empty. The part keeps its place in the side's reading order and says
why, in a full sentence, with the one thing that would change it.

## Two kinds

- `absent` — absent **by design**: nothing is missing, nothing is late.
  Without the subscriptions, supply earns nothing directly. No frame, ink,
  the part's own eyebrow (« L'argent · août 2026 ») so the board's
  structure stays, then a title (« Les vendeurs ne paient pas : l'offre ne
  rapporte rien en direct »), what the side does instead (« Tout le revenu
  de la place de marché, ce sont les commissions, côté demande. Ce que font
  les vendeurs se lit dans le taux de service (plus bas, avec leur
  funnel). ») and why there is no "What if?" either. One quiet action: the
  setting that would change it.
- `pending` — **not yet**: the diagnosis needs team targets. The system's
  dashed edge (dashed = not yet, `--engine-pending-edge`), never red —
  nothing holds the side back that a target names. Title « Pas assez de
  cibles pour nommer ce qui freine les vendeurs », the one target there is
  and its value, one quiet action to the targets.

## Not

- Never a "?" box: a "?" is a figure that cannot be computed (constraint
  4); here there is no figure to compute.
- Never red, never an empty card, never a zero (« 0 € d'abonnements » would
  read as a failing stream).
- Never a comparison with the other side: it may say where the side's work
  shows (the fill rate), never how it compares.

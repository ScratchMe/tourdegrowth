SlideStreams — design system extension 10. The deck's total slide, as a ledger.

Question 10. The only slide about both sides, and it is a **sum** (C70):
"two streams, one total". The hybrid's total slide (screenshot 15) sets its
two engines in two cards, face to face, with an arrow between: a
marketplace must not. This one is an addition read down.

## Rules

- One row per stream, always the commissions then the sellers'
  subscriptions — the order of the sum, never of their size — then the
  total's row under a solid ink rule (`--market-ledger-rule`), heavier.
- Each column is an addition: this month (facts, to the unit), new a month,
  in 12 months at today's pace (projections, ~ and two significant digits),
  and, when any what-if moved on either side, in 12 months with them.
- Every stream figure in one face and one size. **No bar, no share, no
  percentage of the total, no chart**: any of them would rank the two.
- No "+" or "=": they are a disclosure's glyphs (return 07, as TotalBand);
  the rule says "sum", as a ledger does.
- Under each stream's name, where it is read on its own: « les acheteurs ·
  slides 2 à 5 ». The note says it once more: a sum, never a comparison.
- The slide's title is the given sentence (« La place de marché rapporte
  58 954 € par mois : 32 854 € de commissions, 26 100 € d'abonnements des
  vendeurs. »), its stressed words in bold ink.
- Without the subscriptions there is no total slide: one stream is not a
  sum.
- Drawn on the deck's 1 920 canvas with the slide type tokens; scaled whole
  on a phone.

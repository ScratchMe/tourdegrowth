HowItCompares — design system extension 07. Reference and target: one object.

Q9 and Q12. Today: a "Reference" strip from 0 with a dashed range and its
caveat; a "Target for …" box in a separate first step, before any number;
"below the target" on the board. Here, one block under the value:

- **one chart** — BulletChart, with the `band` delta: the bar is your
  figure, the **red tick** your team's target, the **bracket under the
  track** the published range;
- **one legend line**, each mark drawn as in the chart;
- **the caveat**, word for word from the catalogue;
- **the target box**, on the numbers that can name a stage, with "Only a
  target names the stage that holds you back. Without one, the figure still
  counts." and the `?` for "target".

## The diagnosis rule (C1), in the component

- The verdict tag exists **only against a team target**: "Below target"
  (Tag `alert`, the red of a diagnosis) or "At or above target" (neutral).
- A reference **never** earns a tag, a colour or an edge. The band is
  `--viz-axis`, never red.
- Without a target the figure still shows, the reference still situates,
  and no stage is named — on this screen or on the board.

## Rules

- The target is asked **here, once the value is in front of the person**
  (Q12); Settings → Targets holds the same boxes for a team that keeps its
  targets in a sheet. Same field in the model (unchanged).
- The dashed range of today's strip is gone: dashed means "not yet"
  (constraint 7); a published range is not pending.
- No chart when there is nothing to draw (no figure, no range, no target):
  the caveat and the target box remain.
- An estimate: no bar; the legend says "Your figure 2–4 days".

LeverCard — design system extension 07. One lever first.

Q17. "What if?" is folded on today's board and a full screen in the
step-by-step: eight sliders, seven figures, the month's funnel, the
assumptions folded. LeverCard is the way in: **one lever, two figures**.

## Which lever

- The lever of the stage **a team target names** (C1) — with the brief's
  data, activation rate.
- No target: the first lever in funnel order whose number is typed, and the
  title does not mention a stage ("Move one lever and see what follows").
- A lever whose number is not typed has no card (as today: no slider).

## Rules

- The figures come from the engine's own calculation, the one the full
  panel uses: the levers still move together there, with their assumptions
  printed. LeverCard shows two of the seven (MRR in 12 months, new paying
  customers a month); the panel is one tap away: "All 8 levers and what the
  calculation assumes →" (today's panel, unchanged).
- Moved: the title says the move in words, each figure shows "today …"
  under it, "Back to today" appears. Nothing is saved until the person
  saves a what-if, as today.
- The slider is a native range: 44px tap row, 8px track, 28px thumb, ink
  fill to the value; its output prints the value. Keyboard: arrows, as
  native.
- Not a card with a shadow: a ruled section of the board.

EngineStart — design system extension 07. The first visit's setup: one question.

Today "Before you start" asks 36 controls' worth before the first number:
company type, both motions and their windows, the month and the cohort, the
sales-assisted periods, currency, company name, the team's tools, the Tour
link, and four ways in. EngineStart keeps **one question** — how do you sell —
and says every other default in a sentence, with "Change" (Settings).

## Why only this question

How you sell decides which numbers exist (17, 15 or 33): nothing else on the
first screen does. Every other control has a default that is right for most
people and is changeable later (brief Q4):

| Today in the setup | Now |
|---|---|
| Company type (B2B SaaS; two "later") | the sentence ("for a B2B SaaS"); Settings |
| Windows: activation 7/14/30, payment 30/60/90 | Settings ("The month"), with its warning |
| The month of flows, the cohort, the sentence on why | the sentence; Settings; the cohort explained on the denominator of the numbers that use it |
| Sales-assisted periods line | Settings |
| Currency | the sentence; Settings |
| Company name (slides only) | Settings |
| The team's tools (folded) | Settings ("Your tools": they come first in Where to find it) |
| Linking the Tour taken here | Settings; the board's "The Tour and your numbers" |

## Rules

- **Nothing is required**: self-serve is selected by default (today's
  default), so a person can go straight to the first number. The choice is
  a real radio group (`Choices`).
- **One primary**, "Start with your first number →". It opens the first
  number's screen (Sign-up rate for self-serve; the quickest by effort, in
  funnel order — see NextStep's order).
- **The example and import are quiet** (text buttons). "See it all at once"
  is gone: the board is the whole view, and "← Your numbers" leads to it
  from any number. "Enter as a table" moves to the engine menu (File).
- `plan` changes with the motion: the three counts come from the catalogue's
  effort tags (5/7/5 self-serve, 4/5/6 sales-assisted, 9/13/11 both).
- Card: flat (no shadow). On the first visit the page's raised card is the
  privacy promise above it.

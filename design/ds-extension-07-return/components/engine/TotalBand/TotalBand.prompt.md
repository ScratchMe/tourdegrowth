## Rules (C4)

- **A sum, never a comparison**: no bar, no share, no "bigger".
- **Fixed order**: self-serve, then sales-assisted, whatever the values; the
  Segmented keeps the same order.
- No "+" or "=" between figures (those glyphs are a disclosure's,
  constraint 8): the total is set off by a solid rule, as in an account; on
  a phone the three stack and the rule is above the total.
- The link (opportunities from self-serve) is the band's last line, and a
  row in the closed "Computed" group of each engine.
- The small-sample caveat (sales-assisted) sits under the Segmented when
  sales-assisted is shown.

SlideUnitEconomics — design system extension 09. The unit-economics slide, read alone.

Q12. The slide's frame (header, data badge, title, footer, page) is the
deck's own, unchanged. Its body:

1. **six tiles in one row**: CAC, LTV, LTV:CAC (with "an often-cited
   reference: about 3:1" — context, never a verdict), CAC payback, months
   after payback (or "−4 months · leaves ~4 months before paying back"),
   cash tied up ("does not all come back" with the loss; "a floor · monthly
   billing" otherwise). An unknown is a dashed tile, "?" and "missing: gross
   margin";
2. **PaybackChart** under them, and beside it: GRR and NRR in one line (their
   approximation printed with them; they were two tiles), the warning when
   it applies, and what the cash figure assumes.

## The title (C48)

- loss certain: the loss titles the slide — "Each new customer costs us
  €1,900 and brings back ~€1,500: we lose ~€400 on each one." — and the
  slide moves right after the funnel (№ 2); the first slide stays the
  funnel;
- otherwise, today's title (payback and multiple);
- no margin: today's title.

Title figures are **ink**, all of them: red on a slide means the
diagnosis (the stage a target names), as on the board. A gain, a loss, a
multiple are not diagnoses.

## The hybrid

`engines`: self-serve then sales-assisted side by side, each with five tiles
(its months after payback are its chart's) and a compact chart — never
summed: the LTV, the payback, the loss belong to one engine. One shared note
under both.

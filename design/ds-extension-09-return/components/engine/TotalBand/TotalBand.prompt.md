## What extension 09 adds (Q4)

`totals`, under a hairline: **what adds up across the two engines, and only
that** —

- ARR, both engines (a fact: the summed MRR × 12);
- MRR in 12 months at today's pace (the two curves' last points, summed;
  the sales-assisted one is a straight line);
- cash tied up, both engines.

The LTV, the payback, the loss never add (each engine has its own): they
live in each engine's MoneyBlock, under "Engine shown". The per-engine ARR
is not printed (it is the engine's MRR × 12; the board's question is the
total).

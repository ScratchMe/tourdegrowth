Use `Disclaimer` under the actions on every screen that shows a score. It is a product requirement, not decoration.

```jsx
<Disclaimer align="center">A quick estimate, not an audit — <a href="/how-it-works">see How it works</a>.</Disclaimer>
```

Sentence case, not uppercase — it is read, not scanned. Always link to How it works. Do not soften it into marketing copy, and do not hide it behind a tooltip.
